Poker illustrates a principle that applies far beyond the game:

> A good decision is not necessarily one that produces a good immediate outcome. Strong decision-making requires evaluating probabilities, expected value, available information, and long-term results.

This connection between mathematical reasoning and human behavior is particularly interesting to me.

Many of the same analytical concepts can be applied to business strategy, financial research, artificial intelligence, and operational decision-making.
